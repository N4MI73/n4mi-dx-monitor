? Best practices for Watched and Needed triggers

A short checklist. The full version, with screenshots of real triggers, is in the User Guide's Best Practices.

Every trigger

  • Two things must exist: a HamAlert trigger with the Telnet action, and a matching entry on DXMon's Watched or Needed page. Creating one doesn't create the other. An entry with no trigger never shows a hit; a trigger with no entry only adds spot traffic.
  • Review the recipe in HamAlert before saving it. Check the callsign or entity, bands, modes, spot sources and Telnet action.
  • Consider a Spotter continent condition for your own continent. It cuts duplicate and distant spots and keeps busy triggers under HamAlert's daily limit, at the cost of some early reports from elsewhere.
  • Add a comment so HamAlert's trigger list lines up with DXMon's lists, e.g. WATCHED: Nepal (ends 2026-09-19), NEEDED: Glorioso Is., BAND: Singapore 17m, 15m FT8. DXMon doesn't read comments; they're for you.

Watched (a DXpedition callsign)

  • Callsign is the on-air callsign, plus the bands and modes you can work.
  • Compound callsigns (ADXO says "9N", the operation signs "9N/OM0GA"): use HamAlert's Full Callsign condition, and edit the Watched entry to the same full form.
  • When it ends (the Ended flag on the Watched page): remove or pause the trigger and remove the Watched entry.

Needed (a whole entity, or a band/mode slot)

  • DXCC is the entity, picked in HamAlert by the name and number this page shows. The Create HamAlert trigger link on each Needed entry gives the exact recipe.
  • Whole entity: DXCC only. Slot: add Band is and Mode is with exactly the bands and modes checked on the Needed entry (CW, SSB, FT4, FT8).
  • Spelling no longer has to match. DXMon matches Needed entries by DXCC number. Pick the name from the list on the Needed page; an entry flagged "Name not recognised" still matches by name only and needs fixing.
  • Many entities at once: above 5, this builder splits the recipe into Digital and Voice/CW triggers, because HamAlert switches off a trigger that passes its daily spot limit.
  • Once confirmed, remove both the trigger and the Needed entry.
